How this program works

The week is a fixed alternation of 1 strength day + 1 cardio day, repeated every week for all 12 weeks — 7 training days per week, zero rest days. Total: 48 strength sessions + 36 cardio sessions.

Weekly pattern: Mon Strength A (Push) · Tue Cardio 1 · Wed Strength B (Pull) · Thu Cardio 2 · Fri Strength C (Shoulders & Arms) · Sat Cardio 3 · Sun Strength D (Hypertrophy + Core).

Load progression: add 2.5–5% to upper-body lifts (or +1–2 reps) once every set hits the top of its rep range at or below the block's target RPE. Never grind to failure on the no-rest-day schedule.

No-rest-day autoregulation: if resting heart rate is elevated, sleep is poor, or joints ache, drop the cardio to Zone 1 and skip the finishers for that day. The deload week (week 12) is not optional.

Cardio in this program is cardiovascular work, not leg strength training — legs are deliberately excluded.

Cardio days

CARDIO 1 — Easy Zone 1–2 steady state

Tuesday
  • 5 min easy warm-up
  • 25–35 min continuous at a conversational pace (able to speak full sentences)
  • 5 min cool-down
Keep it low-impact and truly easy — it must not compromise Wednesday's pull session. Pick any modality below.
Treadmill Walk
Cycle Ergometer
Elliptical Trainer
Row Ergometer

CARDIO 2 — Intervals / conditioning

Thursday
  • 5–8 min warm-up, then by block:
  • Block 1: 6 × (30s hard / 90s easy) · Block 2: 8 × (30s / 90s)
  • Block 3: 8 × (30s / 60s) · Block 4: 10 × (20s hard / 40s easy)
  • Block 5: 15 min easy only
  • 5 min cool-down
Hard means genuinely hard but repeatable — never all-out on a no-rest-day week.
Treadmill Run
Row Ergometer
Cycle Ergometer
Ski Ergometer
Stair Climber
Stepmill

CARDIO 3 — Bodyweight / mixed-modal circuit

Saturday
  • 5 min warm-up, then 40s work / 20s rest through the circuit:
  • Block 1–2: 3 rounds · Block 3–4: 4–5 rounds · Block 5: 2 easy rounds
Keep one round in reserve — Sunday is Strength D.
